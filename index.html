About me.

I started my journey in game development as a self taught developer with a passion for creating immersive experiences. Over the years, I've honed my skills in various aspects of game design and programming, always striving to push the boundaries of what's possible.

Game Development to be has always been the perfect blend of Arts and Sciences. We're using Science to create Art. Over the years, I've worked on a variety of projects, from small indie games to larger commercial titles, each one teaching me valuable lessons about collaboration, creativity, and technical problem-solving.

My most notable strength stands to be my ability to plan failsafe System Architectures and System Design that leaves no stone unturned in terms of modularity, flexibility and self dependance.

From creating small scale experiences to creating large scale system modules that can be imported to fullfill many menial and heavy tasks at the request of a simple API, I have always strived to create systems with clear abstractions that do not require any pre-requisite knowledge to be used. Systems that are easy to use, easy to understand, and easy to maintain.
